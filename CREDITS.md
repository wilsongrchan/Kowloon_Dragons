# Credits

## Tile sound effect (src/template.html, CLACK_B64)
- **雀ゴロ打牌音 ver.11** ("dahai11") by アンコロキング (Ancoro King) - a live recording of an actual automatic mahjong table with the harsh highs cut. Offered as a free download for Tenhou-style clients: http://ancoro.way-nifty.com/blog/se.html (also bundled, with thanks, in the open-source MIT project 電腦麻將 https://github.com/kobalab/Majiang).
  **Licence note:** the author says "download free" but gives no formal licence text - replace this sound if that is a concern.
  Trimmed to ~0.3 s and resampled to 22.05 kHz mono; played at slightly varied pitch, rising for chow/pung/kong.

Sound hunting also compared against CC0 recordings from Freesound (al.barbosa, Detective_Lemons, Macif, poenia, IENBA) and OpenGameArt (Thwack Sounds by Jordan Irwin, Kenney Casino Audio); none of those are shipped.
