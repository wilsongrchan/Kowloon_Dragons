## Tile sound effects (src/template.html, `CLACK_B64`)
Recorded tile/impact sounds, trimmed, normalised and resampled to 22.05 kHz mono WAV, embedded as base64:
- **Thwack Sounds** by Jordan Irwin (AntumDeluge) - CC0 - https://opengameart.org/content/thwack-sounds (thwack-04, -06, -10)
- **Casino Audio** by Kenney (kenney.nl) - CC0 - https://opengameart.org/content/54-casino-sound-effects-cards-dice-chips (chips-handle-2)

Reference only (not shipped): "Playing Mahjong at Apartment" by al.barbosa and "tiles fall on corkboard" by Detective_Lemons (Freesound, CC0) were used to compare the spectrum of real mahjong tile hits.
